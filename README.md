Sum up 26 November's world news in one image — a dense broadsheet front page:
**38 stories · 11 regions · 14 fields**, every one dated Thu 26 Nov 2026.

- **3 lead stories, drawn:** Houthis claim a strike on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura
  refinery (4 drones downed; damage unconfirmed) · leaked data puts Russian
  war dead at 490,334 (BBC / Mediazona analysis of leaked Rosstat records) ·
  Trump tariffs spook Wall Street (levies proposed on 14 partners; Nasdaq ~-4%).
- **10 more drawn stories:** Cairo tanker fire kills 31 · Israel strike kills
  Hamas chief · Sudan drone strikes · US plane crash kills six · Bangladesh
  garment wage +36% · Colombia 10 years on from the peace deal · Thanksgiving
  turns 100, parade moves to Saturday · London Tube stops · Pyongyang expels
  the Swiss · Korail work-to-rule.
- **25 more stories** in the list, a UTC hour-by-hour timeline, a world map
  with 17 numbered places, how-sure labels (CONFIRMED / REPORTED / CLAIMED)
  on every story, and a DATE CHECK box naming what was left out.

The image is hand-drawn with PIL (`draw_frontpage.py`, fonts bundled in
`fonts/`); the entry program (`main.py`, standard library only) copies the
pre-built PNG to `/output/entry.png`, because the grading sandbox has no
Pillow. It writes only to `/output` and runs identically twice.
